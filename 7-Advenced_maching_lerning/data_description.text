# Thera Bank Personal Loan Dataset - Column Reference Guide

This dataset contains demographic and financial information for 5,000 customers of Thera Bank. 
The primary goal is to build machine learning classification models (**Logistic Regression, 
Decision Trees, and Random Forests**) to predict which existing customers are most likely to accept 
a personal loan offer ($Personal.Loan = 1$).

---

## 📋 Column Definitions & Descriptions

### 1. Customer Demographics
* **`ID`** *(Integer)*: Unique customer identification number.
  * *Modeling Tip:* Drop this column before training; a random serial number has no predictive power.
* **`Age`** *(Integer)*: The customer's age in years.
* **`Experience`** *(Integer)*: Years of professional work experience.
* **`Family`** *(Integer)*: Family size of the customer (e.g., `1` for single, `3` for a family 
with a child).
* **`ZIP.Code`** *(Integer)*: The postal/ZIP code of the customer's residence.

### 2. Wealth & Financial Behavior
* **`Income`** *(Integer)*: Annual income of the customer, typically represented in thousands of 
dollars (e.g., `50` = \$50,000/year).
* **`CCAvg`** *(Float)*: Average monthly credit card spending, typically represented in thousands 
of dollars (e.g., `2.5` = \$2,500/month).
* **`Mortgage`** *(Integer)*: Value of the customer's home mortgage, if applicable, in thousands 
of dollars. A value of `0` means they do not have a mortgage.

### 3. Education Profile
* **`Education`** *(Integer/Categorical)*: The highest level of education completed by the customer, 
encoded numerically:
  * `1`: Undergrad (Bachelor's Degree)
  * `2`: Graduate (Master's Degree)
  * `3`: Advanced/Professional (Ph.D., M.D., J.D., etc.)

### 4. Existing Bank Relationships (Binary Indicators)
*These features use binary encoding:* `0` = No, `1` = Yes.

* **`Securities.Account`** *(Integer)*: Does the customer hold an investment/stock trading 
account with Thera Bank?
* **`CD.Account`** *(Integer)*: Does the customer have a **Certificate of Deposit** account
 with the bank? (Often indicates stable, high-value savings).
* **`Online`** *(Integer)*: Does the customer utilize the bank's internet/online banking services?
* **`CreditCard`** *(Integer)*: Does the customer carry a credit card issued specifically by 
Thera Bank?

---

## 🎯 Target Variable (What We Are Predicting)

* **`Personal.Loan`** *(Integer)*: Whether the customer accepted the personal loan offer during 
the bank's last marketing campaign.
  * **`0`**: **No** (Declined the offer)
  * **`1`**: **Yes** (Accepted the offer)

---

## 🧠 Machine Learning & Modeling Checklist

1. **Multicollinearity (Crucial for Logistic Regression):** Look closely at `Age` and `Experience`.
 They are highly correlated (as age increases, experience naturally increases). Consider dropping 
 one of them or using a dimensionality reduction technique to keep your LR model stable.
2. **Feature Scale (Logistic Regression):** Columns like `Income` and `CCAvg` are on completely 
different scales than binary columns like `Online`. Standardizing or normalizing these numerical 
features is highly recommended before fitting an LR model.
3. **Tree-Based Strengths (Decision Trees / Random Forests):** Your tree models will easily capture 
non-linear relationships. Expect features like `Income`, `CCAvg`, and `CD.Account` to show up with 
high **feature importance**, as wealthier customers with existing trust in the bank are historically 
 the most likely to borrow more.
4. **Evaluation Metric (AUC/ROC):** Because the vast majority of bank customers say "No" to loans, 
your target variable (`Personal.Loan`) will be highly imbalanced (many more 0s than 1s). Standard 
classification accuracy will be misleading. **AUC (Area Under the ROC Curve)** is the ideal metric 
to evaluate and compare your models.
